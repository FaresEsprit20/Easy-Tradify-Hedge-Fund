Definitions

Spread stop — stopped on the bid/ask while the mid price never reached the stop. Noise stop — stopped, then price went on to the original target before moving 3R against. Gave back — reached +1R first, then stopped. Wrong direction — stopped and kept going 3R further against.

Direction right — which barrier of 5× M1 ATR price touched first, within 8 hours. For a directional component it is judged in the component's direction; 50% is a coin. Lift — trade direction right when the component agreed minus when it disagreed; this is the number that says whether a component can separate good entries from bad ones.

Verdicts — ADDS EDGE: lift positive with p < 0.05. PREDICTIVE: direction accuracy CI above 50%. INVERTED: accuracy CI below 50%, or lift negative with p < 0.05. NO EDGE: indistinguishable from a coin. With ~70 directional components, expect about three p < 0.05 results by chance; the trend group's results are consistent across both halves, which chance rarely produces.

Replay check — the stored stop and target re-run on the ticks reproduce the booked result on stop/target closes. That agreement is what makes the exit grid trustworthy.
